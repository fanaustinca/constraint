The cursor
In Rebuild it is loose in the sheet with you. It picks a piece of geometry, right-clicks it, and deletes it — floor, roof, whatever is next. The selection box and the open menu are your warning. Get off that piece.
Violet pads
Mirror pads. Touch one and gravity flips immediately — no button. They come in pairs: one on the floor to send you up, one on the ceiling to set you back down. Ribs hanging from the ceiling keep an inverted run inside the section it belongs to.
Green flags
Datums. Touch one and it becomes your respawn point — sheets are long, so they matter. The strip along the top shows where they are and how far you've come.
Cyan chevrons
Springs. Three tiles wide, and they throw you far higher than any jump — high enough to top walls nothing else reaches.
Pale blocks
Polished faces. Solid, but there is nothing to grab — no wall slide, no wall jump. If one is in your way, a spring or an updraft is the way over it.
Cyan updrafts
Rising air. Walk in and it carries you up; steer out of the top onto whatever the climb was for.
Blue / ghosted
Phase blocks. They trade places on a timer — watch them flicker before they switch.
